# Gameplay Systems

## Core Loop

1. Collect income generated by owned businesses.
2. Reinvest currency into shop upgrades.
3. Meet progression conditions to unlock additional businesses and regions.
4. Recruit managers and develop their levels and stars.
5. Build a team of up to six managers to apply roster-wide bonuses.
6. Return after time away to claim capped offline earnings.

## Business Catalogue

The design catalogue contains 30 businesses grouped into six regions, with five businesses per region. The current runtime unlocks only a subset. Additional catalogue entries and later-region gates remain candidates until their economy parameters and unlock conditions are approved.

## Manager Roster

The current target roster contains 18 managers:

| Rarity | Count |
|---|---:|
| Common | 4 |
| Rare | 5 |
| Epic | 4 |
| Legendary | 3 |
| Mythic | 2 |
| **Total** | **18** |

Managers have four ability dimensions:

- **Business Income** — improves the value of completed orders.
- **Upgrade Discount** — reduces eligible shop upgrade costs.
- **Offline Earnings** — improves offline settlement.
- **Customer Traffic** — increases potential order demand, subject to shop capacity.

Up to six managers can be selected as one team. Team bonuses apply globally to eligible owned businesses.

## Recruitment

The design includes ordinary and advanced recruitment pools, independent pity counters, duplicate shard rewards, and recruitment-related progression rewards. The current probability rules and pity behavior are documented in the project source and should be treated as version-specific.

## Time-Based Systems

The prototype includes offline settlement, recurring tasks, sign-in progression, rotating business campaigns, and timed business events. UTC-based calendar boundaries are used for selected recurring systems to keep resets consistent across regions.
